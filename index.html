Problem statement

Borrowers stuck. Lenders idle.

College students cannot borrow instantly for small needs, and idle cash earns them nothing.

  • No instant loans for necessities and emergencies — borrowers stuck
  • No place to invest spare money — lenders idle
How it works

Four steps from request to ledger update

A simple, transparent flow that keeps borrowing and lending entirely among classmates.

01

The request

A student posts a need — "short 300 bucks for college fest tickets, payback by Friday."

02

The match

Another student from the same campus views the feed and taps "help out."

03

The settlement

The app displays the borrower's public UPI ID or phone number. The lender pays directly via Google Pay or PhonePe.

04

The ledger update

Both users return to the app and tap confirm. The app logs the transaction and updates their active balance profile.

Our USP

Student IDs unlock credit scoring

Student ID login exposes academic and contact data lenders can trust. Sign-in verifies enrollment and identity instantly — giving lenders a trust signal borrowers never had before.

  • Instant enrollment & identity verification
  • A trust score built from real repayment history
  • Only verified classmates on your feed
What about security?

Addressing the one major concern

We built safeguards for both sides of every transaction.

01

No rate changes, ever

The lender cannot increase interest after the transaction is made — Laterpay stays zero-interest by design.

02

Closed once paid

Once the payment is done, the ledger registers it as a closed transaction.

03

Borrower trust score

We score borrowers based on their previous transactions and display it on their profile, so lenders know if a borrower is safe.

04

Capped first asks

First-time borrowers are limited to asks under ₹99, to safeguard lenders from defaulters.

05

14-day grace period

If a borrower does not repay 14 days after the agreed deadline, their account is permanently terminated and a fine is registered on their name.

06

Non-custodial by design

Laterpay never holds or transfers money — settlement happens directly between students over UPI apps they already trust.

SWOT analysis

A clear-eyed view of the opportunity

Strengths

  • Non-custodial model avoids heavy financial regulation and banking licenses
  • Zero fees keep loans ethical, protecting students from predatory debt traps
  • Campus trust: a closed physical community increases social pressure to repay
  • Low maintenance: a simple digital ledger keeps costs very low

Weaknesses

  • High default risk — no collateral, credit checks, or legal backing
  • No interest or fees makes long-term self-sustainability difficult
  • Unpaid loans can create social friction and toxic dynamics
  • User turnover as students graduate every year

Opportunities

  • University partnerships as part of financial literacy programs
  • Value-added tools: budgeting, expense splitting, credit-building
  • Network growth: easy replication across universities nationwide

Threats

  • Regulatory shifts could classify P2P tracking as unlicensed intermediation
  • Students can easily substitute mainstream payment apps or cash
  • A few major scam cases could damage user trust permanently

The raise and the plan

Over the next 12–18 months, our raise splits across product build, campus onboarding, and integration. Each milestone signals readiness for the next round. Progress is tracked live by campuses onboarded, transaction volume, and number of users.
