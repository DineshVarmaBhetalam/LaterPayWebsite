Group rules

📌 How lending & borrowing works in the group

Every request and match happens across two simple groups — General and Requests. Follow these steps so everyone stays safe.

Directions for borrowers

  1. 1️⃣ Submit your request in the "General" group.
  2. 2️⃣ Your request will be posted within 3 hours in the "Requests" group.
  3. 3️⃣ Interested lenders will privately contact you.
  4. 4️⃣ Discuss and finalise the terms mutually.
Note: All transactions must be conducted privately.

Directions for lenders

  1. 1️⃣ Check the "Requests" group.
  2. 2️⃣ Interested lenders must privately message the borrower.
  3. 3️⃣ Finalise terms mutually before transferring money.
  4. 4️⃣ Notify admins once the deal is completed.
Note: All transactions must be conducted privately.
Please format your requests this way, and post them in the "General" group:
Request
Name:
Reg. No:
Amount:
